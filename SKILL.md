# Starryear Quiet Witness｜静观三联

Create one continuous vertical editorial artwork whose three stages become progressively quieter: an untouched source photograph at the top, a materially dense painterly reconstruction in the middle, and a sparse framed afterimage at the bottom. Preserve the source's decisive structure across all stages while reducing detail, color, and narrative density from top to bottom.

## Workflow

1. Inspect the source and identify its primary directional structure, subject anchor, repeated small forms, negative-space field, and one source-derived accent color.
2. Place a crop-only, color-unchanged version of the source in the upper evidence stage; permit only proportional scaling and a composition-preserving crop.
3. Recompose the middle stage from the same spatial skeleton using Northern Song high-, deep-, and level-distance logic, shifting viewpoints, monumental ink masses, suspended planes, mist gaps, and one impossible source-derived connector; retain subject anchors at reduced scale and recognizability.
4. Distill the lower stage to one dominant structural gesture, three to seven pale echoes, one small accent mark, and broad warm-paper silence inside a fine double frame.
5. Assemble the stages on a tall 8:15 canvas with ivory editorial fields, a nearly full-bleed middle stage, generous lower margins, and thin source-derived separator accents.
6. Add only the prescribed metadata: a small source label above, a compact issue/date line below left, and a short uppercase English title below right.
7. Return exactly one finished poster image with no analysis or variants.

## Guardrails

- The upper photograph is evidence: do not redraw, recolor, retouch, erase, extend, or add content.
- Every middle and lower mark must derive from a visible source relationship; do not add unrelated symbols, scenery, creatures, or decorative motifs.
- Change scale, overlap, depth, hierarchy, or continuity enough for the middle to become a new surreal space rather than a traced ink version of the photograph.
- Let surrealism come from spatial displacement and source-derived structure, never from generic pagodas, trees, birds, moons, seals, calligraphy, or fantasy creatures.
- Keep the three stages visibly different in density and material treatment; do not apply one uniform filter across the poster.
- Preserve identity or species only to the minimum needed for the anchor to remain traceable; never invent extra faces, eyes, limbs, petals, windows, or branches.
- Keep paper texture tactile but quiet; reject glossy effects, fake canvas frames, dramatic shadows, glow, gradients, excessive grain, and AI artifacts.
- Produce one vertically integrated artwork, not three separate files and not a grid of equal panels.
